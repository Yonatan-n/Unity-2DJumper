How far will you go?

A casual Infinity runner about a rabbit with a twist (guns!), I used random assets from the internet.

Recorded SFX: https://johnatan-n.itch.io/pistols-reload-sfx

Unlock permanent upgrade, discover new enemies, acuaire different guns, and complete all achievments!


Controls (both work at the same time):

Touchscreen / mouse: Left button to jump, right button to shoot.
Keyboard:  you can use spacebar to jump, and `F` key to shoot.
